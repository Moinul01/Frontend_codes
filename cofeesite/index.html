ABout Peter parker and Jean grey

The steam from two cardboard cups swirled into the crisp autumn air above Central Park. Peter Parker pulled down his mask just enough to take a warm sip, letting out a contented sigh. Beside him on the stone ledge, Jean Grey smiled softly, resting her chin in her hand. Her vibrant auburn hair caught the amber glow of the setting sun, and without a single spoken word, the empty coffee cup beside her gently floated up, folding the cardboard neatly before dropping it into a nearby bin. Peter chuckled, shaking his head. "Show-off. Some of us actually have to use our hands." Jean’s eyes twinkled with playful mischief as she leaned in closer, resting her head gently against his broad shoulder. "Efficiency, Peter. Besides, I wanted both hands free." He wrapped a comforting arm around her, pulling her close. Amidst the chaos of saving the world, this quiet moment felt like absolute magic.
